## AI-tool statement

Neither the downloaded camera-ready nor anonymous AAAI-27 LaTeX guide contains
a requirement for an AI-tool-use disclosure paragraph. The guide's Ethical
Statement is explicitly optional and unnumbered. No AI-assistance paragraph
has therefore been invented or added to this proof.

The separate [AAAI-27 CFP](https://aaai.org/conference/aaai/aaai-27/main-technical-track-call/)
permits judicious generative-AI use and retains author responsibility. The
[author policies page](https://aaai.org/conference/aaai/aaai-27/policies-for-aaai-27-authors/)
also addresses AI authorship and generated text. These are separate from the
formatting kit; workshop-specific instructions should govern any additional
declaration. This audit does not assert that a workshop-specific declaration
is unnecessary.
